The problem

Today's problem shows up in next month's report

Saudi dealerships run on disconnected systems and spreadsheets. By the time a manager sees the number, the damage is done.

The lead goes cold

Enquiries arrive across phone, WhatsApp, campaigns and walk-ins with no single pipeline and no monitored response time.

The service slot slips

Bookings live in separate systems per branch; the same vehicle gets double-booked, and no-shows are discovered after the fact.

The complaint goes public

No owner, no deadline, no escalation path - so the customer's next step is social media, not the dealership.

The solution

One platform, built around the Saudi dealer journey

Live, not reported

Every figure is computed from actual records the moment the screen opens. No one types a number into a report.

Arabic-first, bilingual

Built in Arabic from the first line, with English alongside - not a translated global product.

Complements, never replaces

Integrates with existing sales and aftersales systems through an API layer. No rip-and-replace objection.

Eleven modules, one database

Leads, marketing, service, parts, accessories, complaints, loyalty, satisfaction & NPS, contact centre, social customer care, and data governance.

What dealers actually buy

A better customer experience - and the revenue that follows it

In this sector the money is in retention: the next service visit, the next car, the referral. Every module is built to protect that.

More leads convert

A buyer called back within minutes instead of days is a sale. Monitored response time on every enquiry, from every channel.

Customers come back

Reminded, booked without a clash, followed up after the visit - the service customer returns for the next service and the next car. Loyalty points make it explicit.

Complaints stay private

Every complaint has an owner and a deadline. It reaches management before it reaches social media - and the customer is called before it becomes a churn.

Satisfaction is measured

A short survey after every sale and service, NPS by branch and department, and the low scores routed to a person, not a report.

Staff are productive on day one

Screens simple enough that a showroom or call-centre employee is working in the first session - not the first month.

Managers see it live

No waiting for the end-of-month report. The dashboard shows today's problem today, in colour, with the owner named.
