The problem

Today's problem shows up in next month's report

Most dealerships run on disconnected systems, spreadsheets and reports that arrive too late. The result: the lead loses interest before anyone calls, the service booking slips, and the complaint grows in silence.

Without DealerPulse

Every department sees only its own screen, management only finds the problem at month end, and the fix comes after the customer has already bought from a competitor or serviced the car somewhere else.

With DealerPulse

Every lead, booking and complaint on one live screen, with colour-coded signals telling you where the problem is right now, who owns it, and what to do next - before the day ends, not before the month does.

Modules

Ten modules covering the full customer journey

From the moment a lead appears to years of loyalty afterwards, every stage has a dedicated module, and all of them read from one database.

Lead management

Every lead from every channel - call, WhatsApp, campaign, showroom - enters one pipeline with a monitored response time, so nobody goes cold on the way.

Marketing and campaigns

Campaigns tied to their real outcome: how many leads a campaign brought, and how many of them actually bought - in riyals, not impressions.

Service appointments

Booking, allocation and automatic reminders, with a guard that stops the same vehicle being double-booked across branches and cities, and progress tracked as it happens.

Parts and accessories

Parts and accessories sales in detail - branch, item, margin - plus the upsell opportunity sitting on every service invoice.

Loyalty programme

Points, tiers and rewards that bring the customer back for the next service and the next car, under clear rules you can measure.

Complaints and CX

Every complaint has a route, an owner and a deadline, with an escalation board that stops a complaint going public before it reaches you.

Contact centre operations

One screen for the agent and one for the supervisor: inbound and outbound with monitored handling time, and a direct handover to the owning department with no dropped calls.

Satisfaction and loyalty

A short survey after a sale and after a service measures satisfaction with the service, while NPS measures how ready the customer is to recommend you - scored by branch and department. Anyone who scores low is called before it becomes a complaint.

Analytics and dashboards

Live dashboards at every level: the employee sees their day, the supervisor sees the team, and management sees the whole picture in numbers rather than impressions.

Customer experience

The best customer experience in the market, by design

What separates a dealership customers recommend from one they complain about is not luck. It is three capabilities working every day:

Early warning, before the problem starts

Live signals expose a trend while it is still forming: a response time slipping, a branch falling behind, a customer about to walk away. You step in early and from a position of strength, instead of meeting the problem as a complaint.

A personal approach to every customer

A 360-degree file for each customer: their vehicles, history, preferences and every past contact. So every call starts from full knowledge of who is on the line and what they need now, not from zero.

An experience that improves every month

Satisfaction and likelihood to recommend are measured after every interaction, and the result goes back to the decision maker in that same department - so each piece of feedback becomes a permanent fix in the process, not just an apology.
